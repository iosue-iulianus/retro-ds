**RetroDS**: your favourite classics, with the second screen they never had. Not a Nintendo DS emulator.

RetroDS plays games from older consoles on dual-screen Android handhelds, made for the Retroid Pocket Duo. The game runs on one screen; the other shows the game's own map, items or status, kept current as you play, the way the DS Castlevanias use their second screen. Super Nintendo games run now (Super Metroid and A Link to the Past have their second screens); the PlayStation is next.

RetroDS is in early development. It was called SNES DS until November 2026, and is a fork of [GBA DS](https://github.com/kyannon/gba-ds), which does the same for the Game Boy Advance.
