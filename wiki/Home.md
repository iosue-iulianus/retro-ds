**RetroDS** is a Super Nintendo emulator for dual-screen handhelds, made for the Retroid Pocket Duo on Android. The game runs on one screen; the other shows the game's own map or status screen, kept current as you play, the way the DS Castlevanias use their second screen.

RetroDS is in early development and isn't playable yet. It's a fork of [GBA DS](https://github.com/kyannon/gba-ds), which does the same for the Game Boy Advance.
